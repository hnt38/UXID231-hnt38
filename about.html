Band Overview

0.1g no Gosan (0.1gの誤算) is a Visual Kei rock band known for high-energy performances, distinctive aesthetics, and engaging live shows.

Concept & Style

Combining loud guitar riffs with heavy rhythms and energetic audience engagement, the band has built a distinct presence in the Japanese rock scene.
